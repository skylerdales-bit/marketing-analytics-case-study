# Marketing Analytics Case Study
### Promotional Performance, Customer Segmentation & Campaign Optimization

> **Portfolio Project | R • Excel • Statistical Analysis • Marketing Analytics**

## Project Overview

This self-directed case study demonstrates how I approach a marketing analytics problem from raw customer-level data to actionable business recommendations.

Using a simulated promotional campaign dataset, I analyzed customer behavior, campaign performance, and segment-level differences to identify opportunities for improving marketing efficiency and customer targeting.

**Note:** All data used in this project are simulated and were created solely for portfolio demonstration purposes.

---

## Business Question

How can customer and campaign data be used to:

- Identify high-value customer segments
- Measure promotional effectiveness
- Understand differences in customer response
- Improve campaign targeting
- Translate analytical findings into marketing actions

## Key Findings

The analysis identified meaningful differences in promotional response across customer segments and demonstrated how customer-level behavioral data can support more targeted marketing decisions.

Key outputs include:

- Identification of higher-value customer segments
- Comparison of promotional performance across customer groups
- Evaluation of factors associated with campaign response
- Assessment of model discrimination using ROC-AUC
- Marketing recommendations based on observed customer behavior

---

## Business Application

Rather than treating campaign redemption as the only measure of success, this analysis demonstrates how marketers can combine customer value, behavioral patterns, campaign response, and statistical modeling to make more informed promotional decisions.

The same analytical framework could be adapted to evaluate:

- Free-play promotions
- Loyalty offers
- Birthday offers
- Event-based promotions
- Customer retention campaigns
- Cross-property promotional performance
